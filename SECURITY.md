# Security

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please email **security@co3.io** instead of opening a public issue. Tell us what you did, what
happened, and which AI assistant you used. We will confirm that we received your report and
let you know what we find.

Do not test against another company's data.

## How CO3 Portal handles your access

- **No credentials in this repository.** The plugin holds only the address of CO3's server.
  Your AI assistant registers itself with CO3. You sign in with your own CO3 portal account,
  after a consent screen that names the assistant and where the sign-in returns to.
- **Read-only.** The assistant can read what you can see in the CO3 portal, in one company at a
  time, and cannot change anything.
- **Your permissions apply.** Access follows your CO3 portal account. When your access to a
  company ends, the assistant's access ends too.
